The daily rhythm

Speak first.
Shape it second.

Record in the room where ideas arrive. Bring the audio into SoundDraft when you’re ready. Your work moves forward in three quiet steps.

◉

Bring the recording

Drop in an MP3, M4A, WAV, or the voice memo you just made. SoundDraft keeps the original audio with the draft.

Import or record locally
02

Get it into words

Use your own OpenRouter key to turn speech into an editable transcript. Long recordings are handled in resumable sections.
